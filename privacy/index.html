Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Shelfy is a clipboard history app for macOS. Our approach to privacy is simple: Shelfy does not collect, transmit or share any data.

What Shelfy stores

The items you copy (text, links, images, files and colors), the name of the app they were copied from, the tags you create and your settings. This data is stored only on your Mac, inside the app’s sandbox container.

What Shelfy never stores

Items that password managers mark as concealed or transient are skipped.

No network

Shelfy makes no network connections: there are no accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Your control

You choose how long history is kept, can delete any item, clear the whole history in Settings, or pause recording from the menu bar. Deleting the app removes its data.

Accessibility permission

If you grant it, Shelfy uses it only to press ⌘V in the app you are working in after you pick an item.

Questions or feedback? Open an issue on GitHub.
