Support

Shelfy lives in the menu bar. Copy something as usual, then press ⌘⇧V — or click the Shelfy icon in the menu bar and choose “Show History”.

Pasting

  • ← → — choose an item
  • Return or double-click — paste
  • Shift+Return — paste as plain text
  • ⌘1…⌘9 — quick paste
  • Esc — close the drawer

Auto-paste doesn’t work

To paste the selected item for you, Shelfy needs Accessibility access. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and turn on Shelfy. Without it, the item is copied to the clipboard and you can paste it with ⌘V.

Tags

Right-click a card → Tag to add a tag or create a new one. Press Tab to switch between tags. Tagged items are kept forever; manage tags in Settings → Tags.

History and privacy

Choose how long to keep history in Settings → General. “Clear History…” removes all untagged items. Everything is stored only on your Mac, and passwords marked as concealed by password managers are never saved. To pause recording, use the menu bar icon → Pause Recording.

Language

Shelfy follows your Mac’s language. To choose a language just for Shelfy, open System Settings → General → Language & Region → Applications.
